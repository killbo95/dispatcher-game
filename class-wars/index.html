How to play
Move with WASD / arrow keys, or tap the map. Walk over tiles to claim them. Press SPACE or the power button near rivals to bonk them and steal a little territory. Grab glowing power-ups. Biggest territory wins the round.
Prototype note: solo demo uses simulated rivals. Real shared rooms need the included Cloudflare Worker deployed first.
